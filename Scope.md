Scope of Release

This repository contains the Tensor P2 (P2v2.0) Qiskit implementation corresponding to the publication:

Machwaya, D. (2026).
Hardware Demonstration of a Fixed Tensor P2 Kernel: Zero-Entropy Leakage and Stable Coherence Across Dual Microcontroller Platforms.
Frontiers in Physics.
DOI: 10.3389/fphy.2026.1758206

The materials directly supporting the published work include:
- The fixed Tensor P2 kernel implementation
- Associated simulation workflows
- Benchmarking and reproducibility baselines

Any additional documents or materials present in this repository are not part of the formal publication artifact and should not be interpreted as contributing to the evidentiary claims of the manuscript.

This release is intended to support:
- Independent replication
- Benchmarking across systems
- Verification of coherence-stable execution behavior

All underlying frameworks, including Tensor P2 and QTET, remain under the authorship and ownership of Destiny Machwaya.
